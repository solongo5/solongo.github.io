02

About Me

My journey started in consumer goods — sourcing, supplier negotiations, cost calculations. Then I went underground, literally: I spent years doing demand and maintenance planning for a Rio Tinto mining operation, keeping 40+ drill rigs and 120+ mechanics running with zero tolerance for stockouts on critical spare parts. Back then, my tools were SAP, Excel (the advanced kind), PowerPoint, and Canva. I learned that a well-built spreadsheet model can move a $15M budget as much as any fancy dashboard can.

I came back to CPG with sharper instincts and bigger ambitions — this time managing a $20M global sourcing portfolio across 15+ countries, leading a team of four, and owning S&OP planning with Sales, Finance, and Marketing. One of the perks I never took for granted: flying to Molson Coors, Carlsberg, and other world-class breweries and HQs for in-person contract negotiations, and building a category portfolio. Along the way I kept asking the same question: how do I make the case for a decision with data, not just instinct?

That question is what pulled me deeper into analytics. I learned SQL, Python, Tableau, and Power BI on top of the operational playbook I already knew — and eventually machine learning. I've landed somewhere I like: someone who's sat in the warehouse, sat across the table in contract negotiations, and sat in the boardroom — and who can build the pipeline and explain what it means to a CEO, a mechanic, and a sales rep alike.

After years of making calls with imperfect data, I decided to go back and build the technical chops to back up my instincts — that's what brought me to UW's Master of Science in Business Analytics, which I wrapped up this year. Now I'm looking for supply chain or analytics roles where I can bring both sides: the leader who's made the tough call, and the analyst who can now prove it.
